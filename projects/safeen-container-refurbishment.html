10ft Offshore Container Refurbishment

Structural refurbishment sequence including blasting, repair, NDT, proof-load testing, coating and certification support.

Scope overview

Controlled refurbishment from surface preparation to certification.

The project scope covered complete refurbishment of a 10ft offshore container and supply of certified lifting accessories. The work sequence was planned to allow structural inspection and NDT before proof-load testing and final coating.

Work sequence

  1. Surface preparationSa 2.5 grit blasting of the container, including critical structural and load-bearing areas.
  2. Structural repairPad-eye modification/repair, I-pad repair and fabrication work using approved material requirements.
  3. NDT inspectionVisual inspection and MPI / MT by certified NDT personnel to check pad-eye welds and structural areas.
  4. Proof-load testFour-point lift / proof-load testing with post-test inspection to verify the integrity of the repaired structure.
  5. Coating & markingsMarine-grade three-coat system and required equipment markings.
  6. Third-party documentationInspection witnessing, tagging/data-plate updates and certification support.
