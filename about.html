About OSS Marine

Marine support built around execution.

Who we are

Responsive by design.

OSS Marine Services LLC operates from Mussafah Industrial City, Abu Dhabi, supporting clients with marine services, repair, fabrication, project equipment and associated industrial requirements.

Our operating model is deliberately practical: one commercial entry point, clearly defined scope, disciplined execution and traceable closeout documentation.

We work across workshop, yard and field requirements, coordinating specialist testing, inspection and third-party certification when required by the project.
