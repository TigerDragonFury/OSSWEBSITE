Our approach

Safety is an
operating system.

HSE and quality are built into how the job is prepared and executed. The level of control is matched to the hazard, complexity and acceptance requirements of the scope.

Where specialist inspection or certification is required, OSS coordinates competent third parties and ensures the results form part of the handover record.

1

Plan

Scope review, risk assessment, resources and interfaces.

2

Execute

Briefings, permits, supervision and stop-work responsibility.

3

Verify

Inspection points, tests, records and controlled handover.
